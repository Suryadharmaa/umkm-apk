# Reference analysis and fidelity

Local working-crop coordinates are source pixels `(left,top,right,bottom)`, right and bottom exclusive, in suffix order. The montages guide hierarchy and layout. Device perspective does not determine Android dp.

- **01 Beranda**, `(87,410,327,1027)` and `(397,509,623,1115)`. Left phone places a white overview within blue, then a bold amount and a light trend panel. Right phone puts identity and shortcut before activity, separated by an advert. Use a short blue region, an inset overview and the shortcut-to-activity rhythm. Omit the advert, points and shop/merchant identity from the reference. Current blue hero and catalog counts leave much of the fold empty. [Existing app dashboard fixture](../../../app/src/androidTest/assets/screenshots/dashboard.png)
- **02 Kasir**, `(85,610,237,881)`, `(291,626,443,934)`, `(490,628,644,950)`. Left phone has quiet search and trailing add buttons. Middle groups editable quantities and anchors total/action. Right gives amount a strong charcoal block and clearly separates payment method, tender and confirmation. Use white/charcoal with a single UMKM blue for the primary action, retaining the current stepper and live cart path. Exclude cards, wallets, promos, unsupported taxes/discounts, stock art and USD. [Existing app cashier fixture](../../../app/src/androidTest/assets/screenshots/cashier.png)
- **03 Keuangan**, `(152,575,342,933)` and `(389,570,577,990)`. Left shows balance, modest actions, tinted period overview and history. Right separates period and plot from recent items with right-aligned amounts. Use a pale-blue ledger account group, clear dates and short rows. Existing tall cards put balances after a primary action. Bring those balances forward. Exclude Visa artwork, credit, exchange and bank sync. [Existing account capture](../../phase-4a/screenshots/saldo-belum-diketahui.png)
- **04 Katalog**, `(68,180,662,1325)` and `(54,1322,678,1467)`. Search and pill controls head white cards with left thumbnails, visible prices and a blue creation action. Keep geometry using a category tile and edit affordance. Dock create above navigation with clearance, replacing the reference overlap. Exclude food photos, stock counts, visibility toggles, sharing, variants, storefront and import.
